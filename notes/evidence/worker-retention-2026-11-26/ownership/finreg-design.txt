Runtime FINREG userdata-record reclamation design review
2026-11-26; source reference78f117627cdecbe83600d9e1d39c0b0f923459c1
Status: source-only proposed protocol; no implementation or tests run.

The proposed active-list design is feasible for the default arena allocator.
Use exact small-storage admission and validated source edges, freeze each
inactive node's next link before any physical splice, and separate logical
retirement from the two proofs needed for reclamation: QUEUED and UNLINKED.
Remove the runtime retired-record root scan. Keep a bounded single-consumer
retired drain, with independent retry/remainder ownership, outside the global
SMR physical writer. Custom lua_Alloc can retain terminal-only reclamation.

State and lifetime invariants

Keep active as the existing monotonic1->0 state. Add separate atomic
monotonic QUEUED and UNLINKED bits, initially0. On default64-bit layout a
uint32 flags field fits the current trailing padding of the32-byte record;
check sizeof/offsetof rather than assuming that on all targets.

Use next's low bit as FROZEN. Every node is suitably aligned. All external
pointer getters strip that bit; protocol helpers also expose the raw word.
Only an inactive node may become FROZEN. FROZEN is never cleared for that
incarnation. No physical unlink may bypass a node whose next is not FROZEN.
Every predecessor-link CAS expects an untagged word, so freezing and link
mutation arbitrate in the same atomic location. A separate active load cannot
provide this property: a predecessor can become inactive after that load.

The unique winner of active1->0 owns eventual retired-stack publication.
Helpers may freeze and unlink without waiting for that publisher. A successful
physical splice publishes UNLINKED with a release atomic OR while retaining
the victim lease. A failed splice, failed search, active0, or FROZEN alone
does not prove UNLINKED. QUEUED is published with release OR after the unique
retired-stack push succeeds. Use OR, not stale whole-word stores, so the two
actors cannot overwrite each other's proof. A node is never reactivated or
reinserted on the active list; a later registration allocates a fresh node.

No node can be freed until it is privately owned by the retired consumer,
active0, QUEUED, and UNLINKED. Physical freeing uses the existing routed raw
free; exact storage leases held by paused actors can make that free late,
without authorizing an overwrite or invalidating their final accesses.

Admission is arena-counted. Holding2-3 FINREG node leases can retain up to
2-3 fixed64KiB arenas and their other allocations, not merely2-3 records.
Later arenas must reclaim independently. Generic mem_lease's custom-allocator
no-op is insufficient for runtime freeing; retain that path until teardown.

One shared active-list walk protocol

Use a helper carrying prev, current, next plus at most three linear leases.
For default allocations, acquire with lj_gc2_small_lease_try(g, p,
sizeof(GC2FinRegUDataNode), 0, lease, NULL). It is nonmarking and does not
retain a global SMR read. Never inspect a target's fields before its lease.

Start: load head pointer, acquire its lease, then reread head. Only equality
authorizes interpreting the node. On failure/change, release and restart.
Do not cache obj/active/next across acquiring the target: an address may have
been recycled into a different current head, which must be read anew.

Advance from an active current node: read its untagged next, acquire successor
lease, and recheck the exact next word and current's active/unfrozen state.
Only then transfer current into prev and successor into current, releasing
the older prev lease. At every iteration revalidate the authoritative source
head or untagged prev->next before semantic work or a structural CAS. A leased
but unlinked predecessor is readable storage, not authority for a successor.

Inactive current: first freeze its next by CAS. Do not use the frozen next as
an authoritative identity by itself. Acquire the prospective successor lease,
then validate the source head==current or the retained predecessor's untagged
next==current, and recheck current's frozen next. Only this validated edge
permits CAS(source,current,successor). Keep successor leased through the CAS.
After success publish current.UNLINKED; after failure release/restart/help.

This addresses the detached-predecessor race: every physically removed node
has a frozen next, so a later CAS that expects an untagged predecessor word
cannot succeed through that detached node. If the predecessor becomes inactive
but has not frozen yet, either the splice wins first and the freezer observes
its updated next, or freezing wins and the splice fails. No stale outgoing
edge can certify the wrong node's unlink.

One-node splices are enough. They preserve the relative order of active
records. Consecutive inactive nodes can be helped one at a time; do not skip
an arbitrary unleased chain. Cap structural attempts in GC quanta and release
leases before a mutator retry/poll/throw. A structural restart is not semantic
absence and must not be converted into an absorbing NO_RECLAIM condition.

Fresh active-head publication

Keep the newly allocated node privately owned and leased through its final
post-publication access. Initialize obj/active/flags/next before publication.
Load expected head, lease that exact head, revalidate head, initialize the
fresh next, then CAS head. CAS failure releases/retries the old-head admission.
On success the active list owns the fresh node; the publisher's own lease
protects it if another actor immediately retires/unlinks it. Release both
leases only after all post-CAS work finishes. No Lua allocation or throwing
operation should occur while these transient walk leases are retained.

Retirement and retired-stack publication

Retain a node lease before clearing obj/claiming active1->0 and keep it through
retired push, QUEUED publication, counter update, and progress wake. Losing
active1->0 does not publish the node a second time. The winner may pause before
push; helpers still freeze/unlink it and publish UNLINKED. It remains owned by
that bounded paused publisher until publication completes.

The retired stack is an MPSC ownership-transfer stack, not another semantic
discovery list. Its unique publisher initializes retired_next before the head
CAS and never changes retired_next after that successful CAS. Keeping a lease
on the expected retired head through push is conservative and consistent with
the source-validation rule. The publisher's own node lease is essential for
its post-CAS flag/counter/wake operations.

A consumer may detach a node after the head CAS but before QUEUED is visible.
It must retain such a node for retry. Publish a wake after QUEUED and after
UNLINKED so either delayed event reschedules a previously incomplete record.
Neither pending metadata proof should prevent the GC cycle from closing or
make later independent nodes wait for the paused publisher/helper.

Retired consumer and removal of the retired scanner

Remove the mark-only retired walk at lj_gc2.c9206-9224. Its own comment says
it does not root node->obj. These are PLAIN explicit-lifetime allocations:
block/list ownership retains them independently of mark[], and no production
plain sweep uses their lack of a semantic mark to collect them. Runtime
traversal of the retired stack is unnecessary once ownership is explicit.

This removal matters: an atomic exchange detaching the public stack does NOT
by itself make an external scanner's leased predecessor a safe source of its
retired_next. The private consumer could free/recycle its successor. Retaining
that scanner would require a separate invalidation protocol; leases alone
plus a stable pointer in a detached predecessor are insufficient.

Use the existing GC worker/list-owner serialization for one bounded consumer,
not a traversal-long global SMR reader or physical-reclaimer scope. Under
worker_active the TG allocator directory cannot transfer, so ordinary
lj_mem_free can route a privately owned record to the original allocator.
It already arbitrates with arena readers. Holding global SMR exclusion for
each metadata free would let unrelated paused SMR readers stall all frees.

Detach a public batch and charge every examined node against the work budget.
Save each next link before repurposing/freeing that node. Persist an unfinished
batch between quanta. Keep incomplete-proof nodes on a worker-owned retry list
instead of republishing them to the public stack. A retry FIFO with head/tail,
plus a batch remainder, gives constant work and prevents a permanently blocked
head from hiding eligible successors. Allocate turns fairly between fresh
batches and retry candidates. No runtime root scanner follows those private
lists; their explicit ownership is sufficient for PLAIN storage.

Only free once after both flags acquire true. The consumer may release its own
temporary lease before the routed free because its private-list ownership now
uniquely owns that final free; retain any other required authority until the
last body read. External helper/publisher leases naturally cause late free.
Never dereference the node after free/release. Callbacks are not invoked by
this consumer. A deferred record is bookkeeping debt, not semantic GC work.

Preserving active-node marking without a broad outer reader

Remove the FINREG scan call from gc2_scan_pending_roots at9234. Leave all
unrelated scanners in that function alone. Invoke an active-only FINREG walk
immediately after gc2_scan_global_roots releases SMR at10240. This is a precise
call-site move; it does not require changing the other global root walkers.
Keep g alive under the enclosing VM/GC operation as before.

Use the same leased/validated active-list walk. If preserving current raw
mark diagnostics, reuse the already-held small admission through
gc2_mark_small_cell_admitted (declaration1478, implementation17346), rather
than acquiring another global SMR lookup. A node that retired concurrently
is a structural skip/restart, not a lost semantic object root. Do not mark
node->obj from this metadata-root walk; that would suppress finalization.
Alternatively removing all mark-only FINREG metadata scanning is physically
sound for PLAIN records, but retaining the active scan is the smaller semantic
and diagnostic change to review.

Semantic finalization and ordering

Do not let a helper turn metadata retirement into callback discovery. Preserve
the current finalizer logic: hold the exact userdata body through type/mark/
metatable checks and main-root unlink, leave node active on lookup or root-
unlink uncertainty, publish markfinalized and the callback queue before
retiring the side node (14191-14341). A helper retires only an already-null
obj/inactive record, never an active node with an unclassified live obj.

Retain the current newest-registration-first active ordering. Frozen-link
splices remove only inactive nodes; they do not reorder survivors. The callback
queue remains the existing FIFO transfer, independent of retired-record
reclamation order. Head restarts must not enqueue an already-finalized object
twice. Keep existing FINALIZED/FINREG checks, metatable retry behavior, and
the retained object scope through callback publication. Never change a failed
node lease into a successful absent registration/finalizer observation.

Removing/reinstalling metatables must still produce a fresh registration after
the old active node is retired. Duplicate registration behavior for concurrent
mutations of the same userdata is pre-existing; this memory-lifetime change
must not silently strengthen or weaken its semantic contract.

Pause/ABA invariants

- A pause after active1->0 but before retired push retains one bounded node;
  other walkers can unlink it and continue without waiting.
- A pause after splice but before UNLINKED retains that node, never authorizes
  a false free; later unlink and reclaim work proceeds independently.
- A pause after retired-stack CAS but before QUEUED leaves a retryable consumer
  record; the publisher's own lease protects its final stores and wake.
- A held predecessor lease prevents address reuse of that predecessor. Frozen
  next makes a detached predecessor unusable as structural authority.
- Acquire successor before revalidating source. A stale pointer to a recycled
  allocation is not identity. Only a still-valid source edge permits use, and
  all fields are read anew after that validation. A valid head ABA may name a
  newly published incarnation; treat it as that new node, not cached old data.
- Small-storage leases impose a fixed-arena retention bound. They must not be
  replaced with one epoch/global reader spanning the list or a whole batch.

Terminal cleanup

After all mutators/GC workers join, detach and drain the active head, public
retired stack, consumer batch remainder, and retry list. Preserve the existing
duplicate-ownership rule: inactive nodes still reachable from active links are
owned by retired/private storage and must not be freed again by the active
walk. Mask tagged next pointers before following them. Joined-world teardown
must prove no paused retire publisher remains outside those owners; an
unpublished active0 owner would otherwise be an orphan protocol violation.
Custom lua_Alloc keeps all retired nodes until this terminal path. The global
retired/private fields must be initialized/reset, and no helper-local remainder
may disappear on return or an error path.

Required evidence before accepting the implementation

1. Existing userdata finalizer/metatable and ordering coverage: m8 weak/FINREG
   suite; t-gc2-traverse registration, clear/reinstall, false/no-finalizer,
   unstable-metatable and root-unlink retry cases; GC-root pending reanchor
   fixture; userdata constructor/finalizer-registration OOM paths.
2. Replace t-gc2-finreg-udata-roots' retired-survives4GC assertion with active
   survival, held retired-node safety, and release-then-reclaim. Never use an
   unleased old pointer across GC and interpret address reuse as old identity.
3. Deterministic predecessor race: pause with leased predecessor/current;
   retire/freeze/unlink predecessor, then make stale CAS attempt. It must not
   publish UNLINKED for a still-reachable successor or free that successor.
4. Pause after active0/prepush, after unlink/preUNLINKED, and after retired
   push/preQUEUED. In each case create/finalize/reclaim enough later records in
   separate arenas while the paused actor remains stopped. Assert bounded
   retained arenas, exact-once logical free, valid callbacks and completion
   after release. Include one forced address-reuse/source-revalidation case.
5. Public worker census: parked sentinel; repeated short buffer workers; retained
   handles with repeat joins versus dropped handles; explicit GC and worker
   pools0/1/4, JIT on/off. FINREG raw blocks should disappear after proof/reader
   release, then dead empty-arena trim should release physical mappings.
6. Quiescent transfer and lua_close after incomplete metadata proofs are later
   completed; custom-allocator terminal fallback; Linux assert/ASan/TSan checks.
   No private-table first-attachment diagnostic is needed for this scope.
