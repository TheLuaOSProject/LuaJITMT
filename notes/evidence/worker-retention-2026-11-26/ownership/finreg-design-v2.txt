2026-11-26
Source: 78f117627cdecbe83600d9e1d39c0b0f923459c1
Status: source-only proposal; no runtime edits, builds, or tests performed.

Recommendation and change from the first draft

Use exact small-storage leases and a tagged active-next link for every active
walker/publisher. Freeze each inactive node's next before physically splicing
it. Publish only fully unlinked records to an immutable, one-shot retired
stack. Independent consumers pop one record at a time, with no worker_active
requirement. Remove the runtime retired-record root scan; move the active-only
scan after gc2_scan_global_roots releases its enclosing SMR read.

The active1->0 winner no longer owns the eventual retired push. Instead, an
atomic QUEUE_CLAIMED transition after UNLINKED assigns that push to one actor.
Only ready records enter the public stack, so no retry list or pending head is
needed. Parent agreed to this direction on 2026-11-26; implementation remains
unauthorized and separate from the current small-shrink change.

is superseded. Its private full-batch plus worker_active design is insufficient:
a stopped consumer could retain an arbitrarily large detached backlog and
prevent successor consumers from draining later records. Do not implement it.

Motivation and scope

Parent census: 64 retired FINREG records occupy 64 dead-worker plain arenas
after 16 waves of 4 workers. Total retired records are 128, including main-
allocated thread-handle registrations. Evidence:
/tmp/lj-worker-plain-retention-20261126-4u6_n8fl/finreg-baseline.stdout.
This proposal removes a concrete surviving allocation per affected arena.
It does not itself reclaim TG bodies or unmap now-empty arenas.

FINREG records are PLAIN explicit-lifetime allocations. Block/list ownership
retains them independently of mark[], and the existing metadata root scanner
does not mark node->obj. Removing its retired walk drops no Lua object root.
Custom lua_Alloc retains terminal-only physical record reclamation.

State and ownership invariants

1. active remains monotonic 1->0. Retire only after obj is cleared. Its unique
   winner performs logical-retirement accounting once, but need not push.
2. Active next has low-bit FROZEN, set only on inactive nodes and never cleared
   for that incarnation. Every removed node first freezes its own next.
   Every predecessor-next CAS expects an UNTAGGED word, so it cannot succeed
   through a previously detached predecessor. A separate active load is not
   enough: predecessor retirement can race after that load.
3. Add monotonic UNLINKED and QUEUE_CLAIMED bits in an atomic flags word.
   A uint32 flags field appears to fit the current 32-byte x64 record's tail
   padding; verify layout rather than assuming it on all configurations.
4. Only successful validated active-head/predecessor splice publishes UNLINKED
   with release OR while holding the victim lease. Failure, absence, active0,
   or FROZEN alone cannot establish that proof.
5. Any leased actor observing inactive+UNLINKED may CAS QUEUE_CLAIMED. The
   winner owns eventual retired-stack insertion and retains its node lease
   through its final post-publication access. Losers may release and leave.
6. Initialize queue-ready state before the release head CAS. No post-CAS
   QUEUED bit is required: successful pop itself proves publication.
7. Each record is inserted once, never reinserted. retired_next is mutable only
   before successful insertion and immutable afterward. There is no runtime
   scanner of arbitrary retired interior links.
8. A successful pop owns one record and exactly one future routed raw free.
   Other actors may retain leases, causing physical overwrite to defer, but
   no other actor owns a second free.

Before splice, the active list owns an inactive record. Between splice and
queue claim, the splicer owns it and cannot return without completing or
observing another queue claimant. Between queue claim and push, that claimant
owns it. Between pop and raw free, that consumer owns it. Each gap retains at
most one unlisted record per paused actor, never a suffix or batch.

Shared active walk and publication

Carry predecessor/current/successor and at most three linear FINREG leases.
Default admission is lj_gc2_small_lease_try(g,p,sizeof(*node),0,lease,NULL),
which is nonmarking and does not retain global SMR. Do not use generic
mem_lease's marking/reclaim-veto behavior for metadata identity races.
Never read a target body before admission and source validation.

Head start: load head, lease candidate, reread head. Only equality authorizes
field reads. Pointer ABA can name a fresh valid head; read all fields anew.
Advance: sample current's untagged next, lease successor, recheck the exact
next word plus current's active/unfrozen state, then transfer leases. At each
iteration validate the authoritative head or untagged predecessor edge before
semantic work or structural CAS. Readable detached storage is not authority.

To help inactive current, freeze its next, acquire prospective successor,
validate head==current or predecessor's UNTAGGED next==current, recheck the
frozen current-next, then CAS that source to successor. Retain victim and
successor through CAS. Success publishes UNLINKED and completes/observes queue
claim; failure restarts without claiming unlink proof. Every removed
predecessor's next was frozen, so a stale untagged predecessor CAS must fail.
If predecessor is inactive but not frozen, either splice wins first and its
freezer observes the updated link, or freeze wins and the splice fails.

Use one-node splices, not arbitrary unleased chain skips. After success release
the obsolete predecessor lease BEFORE acquiring an expected-retired-head
lease: victim+successor+retired-head remain at most three FINREG leases.
Helpers must not throw or allocate Lua memory with transient leases retained.
Failed lease is structural retry, not semantic absence or NO_RECLAIM.

Fresh active-head publication retains the fresh node through every post-CAS
access. Initialize obj/active/flags/links, load and lease/revalidate old head,
set fresh next, CAS head. Release/retry old-head admission on failure. Other
actors may retire the fresh record immediately after successful publication.
Allocate only after releasing duplicate-search leases, preserving current
no-registry-mutation-before-OOM behavior.

Ready-only retired publication and independent consumers

The QUEUE_CLAIMED winner keeps its node lease, loads/leases/revalidates retired
head, writes private retired_next, and CASes head. Before success it may rewrite
retired_next on retry; after success it never does. A claim cannot be abandoned
because of contention. Keep the own-node lease through counters and progress
wake. Publish the existing worker wake after insertion.

Pop one head: load; lease; reread head; read immutable retired_next; CAS head
to that successor. A failed CAS releases/retries without dereferencing its
newly returned pointer. A successful CAS owns exactly that head. The protected
head cannot be reused, and the same incarnation is never reinserted, ruling
out Treiber head ABA. CAS success proves the saved outgoing edge remains the
right successor. The pop never reads successor payload, so it does not require
a separate successor lease; a conservative extra lease still needs source
validation. Do not detach a whole stack into private ownership.

After success, save needed fields, release the consumer's own head lease, then
lj_mem_free(g,node,sizeof(*node)), with no later node access. Private unique
ownership keeps it allocated until that call. Other holders naturally cause
late raw free. Releasing the consumer lease first avoids forcing every free
late; freeing while retained is safe but creates avoidable deferred work.
Distinguish logical free counters from physical reuse in diagnostics.

No consumer lock, worker_active claim, private remainder, or retry queue.
Bound CAS attempts/pops per call. Provide independently callable drains at
real attached-mutator and worker progress points, including IDLE. Suggested
hooks: after registration/forget/help releases its traversal leases; ordinary
worker/public GC progress BEFORE shared worker claim. An idle record must not
require a new MARK cycle to obtain a consumer. Review exact callers below.

A pause after active0 permits unlink helpers. A pause after splice but before
UNLINKED retains one splicer-owned record. A pause after queue claim/prepush
retains one claimant-owned record without occupying a public stack position.
A pause after pop/prefree retains one consumer-owned record, while independent
consumers keep popping. A pause after push/prewake can delay that publication's
scheduling; later pushes/wakes and explicit drains still observe the stack.

Attached-actor allocator routing premise and unresolved integration proof

lj_mem_free uses gc_arena_allocd_for_ptr -> lj_tg_find_owner, a raw TG directory
walk (lj_gc.c4764-4787,5973-5983; lj_tg.c1313-1328). A FINREG storage lease alone
does not authorize this from a detached helper. Every new caller must be a
real attached actor with the existing allocator-directory lifetime premise:
secondary mutators retain n_threads/mt_live; registered workers retain
n_workers; the lone main TG is itself the only actor permitted to perform
nonterminal TG reclamation. See tg_reclaim_dead_admissible at1001-1013.
The current collector's worker_active is an additional veto when already held,
but MUST NOT become the metadata helper's exclusive admission. No global SMR
read should span the consumer/list walk. Implementation must audit each exact
new drain entry, including tests and terminal paths, against this premise.

Existing semantic GC phase ownership remains serialized by worker_active.
This proposal does not claim a paused phase owner lets other workers finish
that phase or discover new finalizers. Ready metadata must still drain through
independent mutator/worker entries while that owner is paused. Acceptance must
not hide a new FINREG global gate behind existing collector serialization.

Removing retired scan; moving active scan outside the broad reader

Remove the mark-only retired walk at lj_gc2.c9206-9224. A consumer can free a
scanner's successor even while its detached predecessor remains leased; that
predecessor's retired_next is not a validated source. No replacement runtime
retired or private-batch scanner is required for explicit PLAIN ownership.

Remove FINREG's call from gc2_scan_pending_roots at9234, leaving unrelated
scanners alone. Invoke an active-only FINREG scan immediately AFTER
gc2_scan_global_roots calls lj_gc2_smr_read_leave(g) at10240. This exact move
does not require redesigning other global root walkers. The enclosing VM/GC
operation retains g. Use the shared leased/validated walk. Preserve current
raw mark diagnostics via its held small admission and
gc2_mark_small_cell_admitted (declaration1478, implementation17346), avoiding a
fresh global-SMR lookup. Retirement is structural skip/restart, not lost
semantic object reachability. Never mark node->obj from this metadata scan.

Finalization semantics, retention bound, and terminal cleanup

Retain exact userdata scope through type/mark/metatable checks, main-root
unlink and callback publication. Lookup/root-unlink uncertainty leaves an
active retry locator and existing object/root retry signaling. Helpers never
discover callbacks or retire an active non-null unclassified userdata.
Publish FINALIZED and callback before final metadata retirement. Preserve
newest-registration-first active ordering and existing callback FIFO; record
reclamation order is independent. Structural restarts cannot double-queue a
finalized object. Reinstallation creates a fresh registration. Concurrent
same-object registration semantics are pre-existing, not changed here.

A stopped active walker retains at most three FINREG node leases, publisher
at most two, pop winner one record. Leases count entire 64KiB arenas, so state
the fixed-arena bound rather than a record-byte bound. Existing userdata and
metatable leases have their separate pre-existing bounds. Later arenas and
records reclaim independently; no global epoch pins every future retirement.

The two lists are now DISJOINT: retired insertion requires physical unlink.
After joining all actors, free EVERY node reachable from active head, including
inactive nodes awaiting help; mask tagged links. Then free every retired-stack
node. The old 'skip inactive active-list nodes' teardown rule would leak here.
No private remainder/retry list exists. Joined teardown must prove no unlisted
queue claimant or pop winner remains suspended; check invariants/accounting,
do not guess an orphan pointer. Custom allocators use terminal-only free and
may share the list protocol with no-op node leases ONLY because runtime free
is disabled for that allocator branch.

Alternative if original retirement-publisher ownership must be preserved

Keep pending records on a shared retired list with a SECOND tagged-next
freeze protocol. Independent consumers unlink only QUEUED+UNLINKED nodes;
all predecessor/successor edges require the same leases and validation.
Successful retired splice owns one free. Incomplete records stay public and
must not hide later ready records. Fixed restart-at-head work limits can
starve everything behind a long incomplete prefix; safe per-actor leased
cursors or another explicit continuation protocol are needed for bounded
quanta. This is feasible but adds TG state/teardown work and is not preferred.
Do not substitute the rejected full private batch for this protocol.

Acceptance evidence required before implementation can be accepted

1. Existing m8 weak/FINREG and t-gc2-traverse finalizer registration/order,
   clear/reinstall, false/no-finalizer, unstable-metatable/root-unlink retry;
   GC-root pending reanchor; constructor/FINREG OOM paths.
2. Replace t-gc2-finreg-udata-roots retired-survives4GC assumption with active
   survival, held-retired-node safety, release-then-reclaim and custom terminal
   fallback. Unleased reused addresses cannot prove old-record survival.
3. Stale predecessor pause: freeze/unlink predecessor, then stale CAS must
   fail and cannot falsely mark reachable successor UNLINKED. Forced reuse and
   fresh-head/source validation must be covered.
4. Pause active0; splice/preUNLINKED; queue claim/prepush; pop/prefree. Each
   must permit later registration, finalization and reclamation in independent
   arenas, with bounded retained arenas, exact-once accounting, and completion
   after release. Pop witness uses another consumer without stopped actor's
   worker token. Also test publication/prewake with subsequent independent work.
5. Public sentinel + repeated short buffer workers: retained handles/repeat
   joins versus dropped handles; explicit GC; worker pools0/1/4; JIT on/off.
   FINREG blocks disappear after readers/proofs release, then separate empty-
   arena trimming can release mappings.
6. Joined transfer/lua_close after delayed actors finish; inactive-linked
   terminal cleanup; custom allocator; Linux assert/ASan/TSan. No rejected
   private-table first-attachment diagnostic in this scope.
