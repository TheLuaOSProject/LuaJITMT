## Conditional fairness bound and explicit limits

For a stable captured list of N TGs, repeated invocations that reach the owner traversal, with the target owner's existing authority/semantic guards admitting work, visit that owner within at most N such traversal invocations: each quota/finish exit retains the next current TG ID, zero-work nodes advance within the same finite pass, and every invocation stops after at most one captured pass. With quota1, a target requiring K successful owner quanta receives those turns within at most N*K such invocations under those conditions. The observed four-TG quota1 run needs52 post-return eligible turns and finishes by178calls (<4*52); the measured maximum gap is4. This is a conditional TG scheduling bound, not an unconditional full-collection, raw-cell, handshake, or elapsed-time bound. Earlier graph/string/grace work or a closed authority gate can delay reaching the TG pass.

The existing work quota and one-finished-arena boundary remain. Each owner iteration may include up to64 scan units (cell or certified bitmap word), remote draining, and other work; this patch creates no exact raw-cell bound. Same-TG multiple-arena fairness is explicitly not solved: an earlier held arena may postpone later work owned by that same TG. All work/cursors remain retained. Arbitrary ongoing attach/detach has no stronger fairness guarantee. Lookup adds an optional current-list walk; these observational fixture timings include logging and are not a throughput benchmark.
