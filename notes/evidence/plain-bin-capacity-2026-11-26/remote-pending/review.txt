Conclusion

The ASan failure at t-arena-remote-pending.c186 was a pre-existing incorrect
immediate-drain assertion, not a bin insertion regression or sanitizer finding.
The same legal forced schedule fails drain==8 on both the frozen c106cd7bb
allocator and final bin candidate, and both correctly preserve then reclaim
all eight allocations. The corrected complete fixture passes both ASan builds.

Source mechanism

All eight fixture bodies are TRAVERSABLE allocations in the same arena.
arena_late_claim_release publishes durable late intent before trying lifetime
LIVE->DESTRUCT. arena_destruct_claim_live then requires the exact reader count
own_count=1. Another publisher's admission can defeat that proof. It restores
LIVE and returns; the caller retains block=1 and late=1 without overwriting
the body. That result is successful ownership transfer, not immediate queue
publication. The ordinary owner queue drain does not consume traversable late
bits; PREPSWEEP and quarantine do so under their stronger generation proof.

Deterministic schedule and exact states

The existing post-queue pause hook stops publisher0 after its queue CAS but
before releasing its arena admission. Publishers1-7 run and join while that
one admission remains. Each sees at least one admission besides its own and
therefore retains a late LIVE allocation. After publisher0 resumes and joins:

                          target0           targets1-7
immediate queue drain     block0 late0 FREE  block1 late1 LIVE
PREPSWEEP                 block0 late0 FREE  block1 late1 FREE/FREEING
quarantine completion     block0 late0 FREE  block0 late0 FREE

Immediate drain count is exactly1 in both builds. All seven deferred payloads
remain unchanged before and after that drain. Real allocator operations
prepare_sweep_kind -> quarantine_one -> reclaim_seal -> quarantine_finish
consume the remaining seven intents. No repeated raw free is used.

alloc_black=1 makes explicit free intent, rather than unmarked collection,
responsible for reclamation. A marked live guard remains allocated with its
payload intact. Live opaque fillers exhaust the remaining bump tail. The
next eight allocations consequently reuse the exact original eight addresses,
and the owned list still contains only the original arena.
