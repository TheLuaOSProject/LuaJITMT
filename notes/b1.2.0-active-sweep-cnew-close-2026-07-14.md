# b1.2.0 active-SWEEP CNEW and shutdown coverage (2026-07-14)

## Scope

The b1.2.0 gate now exercises two release-critical compositions which were
previously covered only separately:

- a published native trace performs materialized FFI `CNEW` allocations while
  the real GC2 collector is in an open, bridge-ready SWEEP window;
- `lua_close()` starts while a fresh zero-worker state is still in that same
  active SWEEP shape with physical traversable-arena work pending and a live
  JIT trace still published.

This is correctness coverage, not a claim that the b1.2.1 performance work is
complete.  Exact `CNEWI` allocation-to-snapshot pause coverage also remains a
later strengthening; ordinary `CNEWI` behavior is already covered elsewhere.

## Native CNEW proof

`tests/t-gc2-jit-sweep-coop.c` records a fixed-size POD cdata loop in IDLE
with sinking disabled.  The fixture requires live mcode containing a loop, a
materialized `IR_CNEW`, and later volatile telemetry loads/stores.  Each native
SWEEP hit is counted only after that iteration's allocation has executed.

The active call runs only after observing SWEEP, the completed semantic bridge,
an open JIT phase gate, and the still-published CNEW trace.  It then proves:

- at least one iteration executed natively during SWEEP;
- SWEEP and its entry gate remained open after the bounded 64-allocation call;
- the returned cdata retained its exact fields after SWEEP reached IDLE;
- the same globally rooted cdata retained those fields after a later full GC.

The helper build continues to attribute a later gate close to the common JIT
allocation-accounting checkpoint and drives bounded physical reclaim to IDLE.

## Active-SWEEP close proof

`tests/t-gc2-no-legacy-runtime.c` preserves its existing worker-backed IDLE
shutdown workload and adds a separate fresh state.  Through public Lua APIs the
new state fixes the worker count at zero, records a real numeric-loop trace,
and creates 100,000 unreachable arena objects with automatic collection
stopped.  Public `lua_gc(RESTART/STEP)` calls stop at an observed open SWEEP
whose main TG still owns a traversable `needsweep` or quarantine arena.

Immediately before `lua_close()` the fixture rechecks the phase, bridge, gate,
pending physical arena work, and exact runnable trace with live mcode.  The ordinary and
amalgam builds both close directly from that state.  The fixture intentionally
uses inline state and public APIs for this proof because internal `LJ_FUNC`
helpers have external linkage in split builds but become static in `ljamalg.o`.

## Validation

- `m6_jit_gc2_readiness`: passed, including the new native active-SWEEP CNEW
  proof and the existing TNEW/MARK cooperation fixtures.
- `m3_gc2_no_legacy_runtime`: passed in ordinary and amalgam builds, including
  retired-symbol gates and both IDLE and active-SWEEP shutdown.
- `m6_jit_alloc_account`: passed, including the IDLE metadata-reclaim entry
  race and the helper CNEW/SWEEP and MARK cooperation fixtures.
- The post-HEAD ASAN target previously passed both fixtures directly; the
  registered optimized variants above provide the final normal-build evidence.

The first aggregate attempt exposed a stale assumption in
`t-jit-idle-reclaim-entry`: it froze the exclusive IDLE SMR writer and then
forced failed-ISNEXT JLOOP despecialization, whose trace-body admission
correctly retries until that bounded writer leaves.  The fixture now tests
closed-gate immutable-sidecar
ITERN dispatch while the writer is paused, releases the writer, and only then
tests lease-dependent exact `JLOOP -> ITERC` despecialization.  The separate
startins-sidecar fixture retains the exact live-trace invalidation proof.  This
preserves both proofs without
constructing a circular test-only dependency; the full aggregate then passed.
